📁 How to Manage Projects

There are two ways to access and manage your texture pack projects:

🔹 Method 1: Using Windows Explorer

  1. Press Meta (Windows) + R to open the Run dialog.
  2. Type %appdata%/QMtexturepackmaker/ and press Enter.
  3. Open the projects folder.
  4. Inside, you’ll find all your saved projects. You can rename, copy, or delete them manually.

🔹 Method 2: From Inside the Editor

  1. Open your project in the editor.
  2. Click on File in the top menu.
  3. Select Open Folder in Explorer.
  4. This will open the project folder directly in Windows Explorer.

Managing your projects manually gives you full control over backups, renaming, and organization.
