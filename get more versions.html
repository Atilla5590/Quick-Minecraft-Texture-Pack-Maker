📥 How to Get More Minecraft Versions

To add more Minecraft versions to your editor, follow these steps using the official Minecraft Launcher:

  1. Step 1: Open the Minecraft Launcher.
  2. Step 2: Click on Java Edition.
  3. Step 3: Click on Installations (or Instances depending on your launcher version).
  4. Step 4: Click New Installation (or New Instance).
  5. Step 5: Select the Minecraft version you want and click Create or Install.
  6. Step 6: Once installed, refresh the editor and the version should appear.

After installation, your new version will be available for selection when creating or editing a project.
