📦 How to Export Your Texture Pack

Once your project is ready, follow these steps to export it into a Minecraft-compatible texture pack:

🔹 Accessing the Export Panel

  1. Open the editor for your project.
  2. Click on File in the top menu.
  3. Select Export Project.

🔹 Filling Out Export Details

In the export panel, you’ll see the following fields:

  • Name Textbox: Enter the name of your texture pack.
  • MOTD Textbox: Add a short message or description. You can use MOTD color codes here.
  • pack.png Selection: Choose an image to represent your pack. This will be included in the exported zip.
  • Export Button: Click to generate the filtered, zipped texture pack.

🔹 What Happens During Export

  • Only files listed in opened.txt are included.
  • A pack.mcmeta file is generated with your MOTD and pack format.
  • The pack is zipped and saved to your desktop or export folder.

Once exported, you can drop the zip into your Minecraft resourcepacks folder and activate it in-game.
