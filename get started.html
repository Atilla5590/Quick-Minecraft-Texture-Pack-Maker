🛠️ Getting Started

Follow these steps to begin using the Quick Minecraft Texture Pack Maker:

  1. Step 1: Open the app by double-clicking the .exe file.
  2. Step 2: Click New Project.
  3. Step 3: Select your Minecraft versions. For more information or to download versions, click here.
  4. Step 4: Click New Project again. Your project should automatically open once it's done.
  5. Step 5: You're done!

Need help with exporting? Visit the Export Guide.
