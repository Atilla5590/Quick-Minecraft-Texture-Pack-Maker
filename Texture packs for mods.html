🧩 How to Make Texture Packs for Mods

Mods introduce custom blocks, items, and entities with their own texture paths. Here's how to retexture them using the Quick Minecraft Texture Pack Maker:

🔹 Step 1: Understand Mod Asset Paths

  • Each mod stores its textures in a folder like assets/modid/textures/....
  • To retexture a mod item, you must match its internal path exactly in your project.
  • You can inspect a mod’s .jar file (it’s just a zip) to find its asset structure.

🔹 Step 2: Add Mod Folders to Your Project

  1. Open your project in the editor.
  2. Click File → Open Folder in Browser.
  3. Click Edit to add folders manually.
  4. ⚠️ The first time you do this, a warning will appear. Read and understand it before proceeding.
  5. Create folders like assets/modid/textures/item/ and place your custom textures inside.

🔹 Step 3: Open or Edit the Texture

  • To ensure a texture is included in the export, you must open or edit it in the editor.
  • Only files listed in opened.txt are exported.
  • Use the preview panel to verify your changes.

🔹 Step 4: Export as Usual

Once your mod textures are ready, follow the standard Export Ritual to generate your pack. The modded assets will be included if they were opened or edited.

Need help finding mod paths? Try opening the mod’s .jar and browsing its assets folder.
